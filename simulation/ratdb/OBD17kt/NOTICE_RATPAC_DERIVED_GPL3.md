# Attribution: ratpac-two derived tables (GPL-3.0)

`OPTICS_OBD.ratdb` and `PMT_OBD.ratdb` in this folder are **derived from the stock tables of ratpac-two 4.4.0**
(https://github.com/rat-pac/ratpac-two, GPL-3.0): the LAB/PPO optical properties (SNO+ data shipped with ratpac-two)
and the `r3600` PMT model. They were generated by `../../tools/make_obd_ratdb.py` (read the stock tables from the
pinned container, then rescale/adapt them for the OBD detector; see `../../docs/OBD17kt_DETECTOR_MODEL.md`).

They are distributed under the **GNU GPL v3.0** (the licence of this repository, `../../../LICENSE`), with credit to
the ratpac-two authors. The other files in this folder (`OBD17kt.geo`, `PMTINFO_OBD17kt.ratdb`,
`ENCAPSULATION_OBD.ratdb`, `MATERIALS_OBD.ratdb`) were written for this project.

`IBD_GEO.ratdb` is **deliberately absent**: it is derived from S. Enomoto's geoneutrino spectra, which carry no
licence, so it is never redistributed. Regenerate it with `../../tools/make_geo_ibd_spectrum.py` (see `../../README.md`).
